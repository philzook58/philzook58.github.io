Hi, I'm Philip Zucker!

I'm a physics/computer boy trying to find his place in the world. I try to keep this site fast and loose to encourage posting, sort of in the style of lab notebook. So, it's not always in the most readable form for others. Sorry about that.

If you liked my blog, please do send me an email saying so at <philzuckerblog@gmail.com> and put a pep in my step. Or you can buy me a coffee <https://ko-fi.com/philipzucker>

You can also contact me at `@sandmouth` on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/sandmouth.bsky.social), [Mastodon](https://types.pl/@sandmouth), or [Twitter](https://twitter.com/sandmouth). My Github is at [https://github.com/philzook58](https://github.com/philzook58).
