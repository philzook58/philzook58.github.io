It would be fantastic if a merely technological or UX solution could fix the problems with AI.

Earlier on, we were excited by copilot autocomplete. Low latency feedback felt incredibly useful while keeping you in the driver's seat.
This has it's problems too.

What I think I want is a better delineation of AI work and me work. Feeling friction is important for making something good, but too much friction is pain with little benefit. I think it is mostly ok to let the AI handle build and install issues rather than me randomly typing shell commands I found in a forum.

One delineation could be by file. Some files are never to be editted by a human and some never by AI. Another idea I like is to have a running commentary. With a prompt i can keep a chat live with a running watch command for when I save.

Another possible workflow is to have the AI emulate another person, akin to google docs or vs code liveshare.

I think moving over to the chat window or sidebar is flow breaking.

The frontier models are painfully slow to interact with. The latency is very high and flow breaking. Even though I know I shouldn't, I spend a lot of time empty headed just looking at the little thinking circle.

AI is a bucking bronco and it is very very easy to end up completely over your skis. I never found code review pleasant, and it is less pleasant to try and review AI code.

I want to feel extended and enhanced, not replaced.

I love AI. I love having my phone be able to connect to my computer at home and have it do benchmarking or little prototypes while I talk into the microphone and look out at the pond and the ducks. I have become very engaged with the pond and ducks this year. To look at water is nearly all I want.

But I also feel that it is brainrotting. It is training me to yammer at the AI rather than taking 10 minutes to think for myself. It is much less hard work and it often says things that surprise me in a pleasant way.
